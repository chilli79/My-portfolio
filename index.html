Engineer profile

FILE / PROFILE

I'm an Electronics and Telecommunication engineer who works where circuit design meets the physical system: the board, the wiring, the firmware and the mechanism all have to agree before anything moves or flies.

My hands-on work covers PCB design under tight mechanical constraints, microcontroller-based embedded development, and integrating electronic subsystems into robots and UAV hardware. At Agam Robotics I've worked in a robotics engineering environment on hardware development and UAV telemetry and wireless communication.

I care about the details that decide whether hardware works off the bench: connector choice, board outlines that fit the airframe, clean interfaces between subsystems, and documentation the next engineer can actually use.

PROJECT 01

5-DOF robotic arm on a mecanum-wheel base

A mobile manipulation platform: a five-degree-of-freedom arm mounted on an omnidirectional mecanum chassis, driven and operated over Bluetooth Low Energy.

MY ROLE

Built the system end to end: arm and chassis integration, the electronics and wiring, and the embedded control architecture.

ENGINEERING CHALLENGES

  • Coordinating five arm joints and four independently driven wheels from one embedded controller.
  • Mounting a moving arm on a mobile base while keeping the platform stable.
  • Routing power and signal wiring cleanly through moving joints.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS

  • Mecanum wheels for omnidirectional positioning, so the base can line the arm up with a target without turning.
  • BLE for control: a low-power, short-range link that pairs directly with a phone or laptop.

TECHNOLOGIES

ESP32Embedded C++BLEPWM motion controlMecanum driveMechanical integration

OUTCOME

An integrated, working mobile manipulator with BLE-based drive and arm control.

PROJECT 02

Custom PCB design and hardware development

Three boards, each shaped by a different constraint: size, mechanical fit and test-bench workflow.

BOARDS

  • Distance sensor PCB, 29 × 20 mm. A compact sensor board where every millimetre of placement and routing counts.
  • IMU carrier board. A constrained layout with mechanical cutouts so the board fits its mounting and keeps the sensor where it must sit.
  • Multiplexer-based ESC programming jig. One fixture that routes an ST-Link or J-Link debugger to several ESCs in turn, so they can be flashed without re-cabling each one.

MY ROLE

Schematic capture, component selection, PCB layout and connector planning for each board.

ENGINEERING CHALLENGES

  • Fitting components, connectors and mounting holes inside a fixed small outline.
  • Working around cutouts and keep-outs set by the mechanical design.
  • Switching debug signals cleanly between several targets on the jig.

TECHNOLOGIES

Schematic designPCB layoutST-LinkJ-LinkMultiplexingKiCadDRC · Gerber · BOM
